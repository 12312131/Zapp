How to Remove Image Background for Free (5 Best Methods)

📅 May 3, 2025⏱️ 5 min readHOW-TO

Background Removal Made Easy

You do not need Photoshop to remove backgrounds. These free tools do it in seconds with AI.

1. remove.bg — Best Overall

Upload an image → background removed in 5 seconds. Incredible AI accuracy. Free for standard resolution.

2. Canva — Best for Designers

Built into Canva's editor. One-click background removal. Free users get limited uses; Pro gets unlimited.

3. Photoroom — Best Mobile App

Available on iOS and Android. Tap to remove background → add new backgrounds or make product photos.

4. GIMP — Best Free Desktop Tool

Open-source Photoshop alternative. Fuzzy Select tool or Foreground Select for manual background removal. Free forever.

5. ChatGPT + DALL-E — AI Method

Upload an image to ChatGPT and ask it to remove the background or place the subject on a new background.
