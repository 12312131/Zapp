How to Take Screenshots on Any Device (2025 Complete Guide)

📅 May 11, 2025⏱️ 7 min readHOW-TO

Screenshots on Every Device

Need to capture your screen? Here is every method for every device, updated for 2025.

Windows

MethodShortcutWhat It Does
Full ScreenPrtScnCopies entire screen to clipboard
Save to FileWin + PrtScnSaves screenshot to Pictures folder
Snipping ToolWin + Shift + SSelect area to capture
Active WindowAlt + PrtScnCaptures only the active window
Game BarWin + GGaming screenshots and recording

Mac

MethodShortcutWhat It Does
Full ScreenCmd + Shift + 3Saves to Desktop
Select AreaCmd + Shift + 4Crosshair selector
WindowCmd + Shift + 4 + SpaceClick a window to capture
Screenshot AppCmd + Shift + 5Full screenshot toolbar
Touch BarCmd + Shift + 6MacBook Touch Bar only

iPhone & iPad

  • Face ID models: Press Side button + Volume Up simultaneously
  • Home button models: Press Home + Side button simultaneously
  • Screenshots save to Photos app
  • Tap the preview to annotate before saving

Android

  • Most phones: Power + Volume Down simultaneously
  • Samsung: Power + Volume Down, or palm swipe
  • Google Pixel: Power + Volume Down, or Back gesture + Power
  • Scrolling screenshot: Take screenshot → tap "Capture more"

Chromebook

MethodShortcut
Full ScreenCtrl + Show Windows
Select AreaCtrl + Shift + Show Windows
