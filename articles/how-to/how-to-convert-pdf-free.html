How to Convert PDF to Word, Excel, or Image (5 Free Methods)

📅 May 12, 2025⏱️ 6 min readHOW-TO

Why Convert PDFs?

PDFs are great for sharing but terrible for editing. Here are 5 free methods to convert PDFs to editable formats.

Method 1: Google Docs (Free, Online)

  1. Upload your PDF to Google Drive
  2. Right-click the PDF → Open with → Google Docs
  3. The PDF is now an editable document
  4. Download as .docx, .txt, or other formats

Best For

Simple text-based PDFs. Formatting may not be perfect for complex layouts.

Method 2: Microsoft Word (Free with Microsoft 365 Web)

  1. Go to word.office.com (free with Microsoft account)
  2. Upload your PDF
  3. Word automatically converts it to an editable document

Method 3: iLovePDF (Free, Online)

  1. Go to ilovepdf.com
  2. Choose your conversion: PDF to Word, Excel, PPT, or Image
  3. Upload your file
  4. Download the converted file

Free tier allows 2 conversions per day. No account needed.

Method 4: Preview on Mac (Built-in)

  1. Open the PDF in Preview
  2. File → Export
  3. Choose format: JPEG, PNG, or TIFF

Built into macOS. No download needed. Only works for image conversion.

Method 5: ChatGPT / Gemini (AI-Powered)

  1. Upload your PDF to ChatGPT or Gemini
  2. Ask: "Convert this PDF content to a formatted Word document"
  3. Copy the output or ask it to create a downloadable file

Best for extracting and reformatting text content.
