How to Google Like a Pro: 15 Search Tricks Most People Don't Know

📅 Apr 20, 2025⏱️ 5 min readHOW-TO

Most People Google Wrong

Google has powerful search operators that 99% of people never use. These tricks will help you find exactly what you need.

Essential Search Operators

OperatorExampleWhat It Does
"exact phrase""how to learn Python"Searches for the exact phrase
-excludeapple -fruitExcludes a word from results
site:site:reddit.com best laptopSearches within a specific site
filetype:filetype:pdf machine learningFinds specific file types
before:AI news before:2024Results before a date
after:AI tools after:2025-01-01Results after a date
ORbest laptop OR tablet 2025Either term in results
intitle:intitle:review iPhone 16Word must be in page title
inurl:inurl:blog AI toolsWord must be in URL
*"best * for beginners"Wildcard — fills in any word

Pro Tips

  • Add "reddit" to any search — Gets real human opinions instead of SEO content
  • Use Google Scholar — scholar.google.com for academic papers
  • Image search by upload — Drag an image into Google Images to find similar images
  • Use Perplexity AI instead — For complex questions, AI search often gives better answers than Google
