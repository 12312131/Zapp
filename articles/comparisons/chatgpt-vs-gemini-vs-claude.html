Detailed Breakdown

Writing: ChatGPT & Claude Tie

Both produce excellent prose. ChatGPT is slightly better at following specific formatting instructions. Claude writes more natural, human-sounding text. Gemini is good but tends to be more verbose.

Coding: ChatGPT & Claude Tie

ChatGPT has the edge in debugging and code explanation. Claude excels at understanding large codebases (thanks to its huge context window). Gemini is competent but behind the other two.

Research: Gemini Wins

Gemini has direct access to Google Search and provides up-to-date information with sources. ChatGPT can browse the web but is less seamless. Claude has no web access in its free tier.

Long Documents: Claude Wins

Claude's 200K token context window means it can read and analyze entire books in one conversation. ChatGPT and Gemini have smaller (but growing) context windows.

Image Generation: ChatGPT Wins

ChatGPT has DALL-E 3 built in. Gemini has Imagen but it is more restricted. Claude cannot generate images at all.

Our Recommendation

Which AI Should You Use?

  • For everyday use: ChatGPT — best all-rounder
  • For research: Gemini — Google integration is unbeatable
  • For writing & long documents: Claude — most natural writing quality
  • On a budget: Gemini — most generous free tier

The honest answer: use all three for free. Switch between them based on the task.
