Canva vs Figma: Which Design Tool Should You Use? (2025)

📅 Apr 28, 2025⏱️ 6 min readCOMPARISON

Different Tools for Different People

Canva and Figma are both design tools, but they serve very different audiences.

FeatureCanvaFigma
Target UserEveryoneDesigners & developers
Learning Curve5 minutesDays to weeks
TemplatesMillionsCommunity only
PrototypingBasicAdvanced
CollaborationGoodExcellent
AI FeaturesMagic Design, AI imagesAI plugins
PriceFree + $13/mo ProFree + $15/mo Pro
Best ForSocial media, presentationsUI/UX design, apps

Verdict

Use Canva if you need quick, good-looking graphics without design skills.
Use Figma if you are designing apps, websites, or need pixel-perfect control.
