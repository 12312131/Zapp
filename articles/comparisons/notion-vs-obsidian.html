Notion vs Obsidian: Which Note-Taking App is Better? (2025)

📅 May 10, 2025⏱️ 8 min readCOMPARISON

Two Philosophies of Note-Taking

Notion and Obsidian are the two most popular note-taking apps for power users. But they have fundamentally different approaches.

FeatureNotionObsidian
PhilosophyAll-in-one workspacePersonal knowledge base
Data StorageCloud (Notion servers)Local (your device)
FormatProprietary blocksPlain Markdown files
OfflineLimitedFull
CollaborationExcellentLimited
AI FeaturesNotion AI ($10/mo)Community plugins
DatabasesBuilt-in (powerful)Via plugins
PriceFree + $8-15/moFree (Sync $4/mo)
Learning CurveModerateSteep
CustomizationModerateExtreme (plugins)

Choose Notion If:

  • You work in a team and need collaboration
  • You want databases, Kanban boards, and wikis in one tool
  • You prefer a visual, block-based editor
  • You want built-in AI features

Choose Obsidian If:

  • You want to own your data (local files, no vendor lock-in)
  • You are building a personal knowledge base with linked notes
  • You want maximum customization via plugins
  • You need full offline access
  • Privacy is a priority

Our Verdict

For teams and project management: Notion.
For personal knowledge and long-term notes: Obsidian.
For students: Either works, but Notion is easier to start with.
