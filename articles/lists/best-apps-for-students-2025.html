15 Best Apps for Students in 2025 (Study, Notes, Organization)

📅 May 2, 2025⏱️ 7 min readTOP LIST

Study Smarter, Not Harder

The right apps can transform your academic life. Here are 15 essential apps for students in 2025.

Note-Taking

  • Notion — All-in-one workspace for notes, databases, and project management
  • Obsidian — Linked note-taking for building a personal knowledge base
  • GoodNotes — Best handwriting app for iPad with AI search

Study Tools

  • Anki — Spaced repetition flashcards. The gold standard for memorization
  • Quizlet — Flashcards with AI-generated study materials
  • Forest — Focus timer that grows a virtual tree. Stay off your phone

AI Assistants

  • ChatGPT — Explain concepts, help with essays, solve problems
  • Perplexity — Research with cited sources. Better than Google for academic research
  • Photomath — Scan math problems and get step-by-step solutions

Organization

  • Google Calendar — Schedule classes, deadlines, and study sessions
  • Todoist — Task management with due dates and priorities
  • Google Drive — Free cloud storage for all your documents

Writing

  • Grammarly — Catch grammar errors and improve writing quality
  • Zotero — Free reference manager for citations and bibliographies
  • Hemingway Editor — Simplify complex writing. Make essays clearer
