20 Best Chrome Extensions in 2025 (Productivity, AI, Privacy)

📅 May 8, 2025⏱️ 8 min readTOP LIST

Supercharge Your Browser

The right Chrome extensions can save you hours every week. Here are our top 20 picks for 2025.

Productivity

  1. Todoist — Best task manager extension. Add tasks from any webpage.
  2. Notion Web Clipper — Save any webpage to your Notion workspace.
  3. Momentum — Beautiful new tab page with daily focus and to-do list.
  4. OneTab — Convert all tabs to a list. Saves memory and reduces clutter.
  5. Grammarly — Real-time writing assistance everywhere you type.

AI Tools

  1. ChatGPT Sidebar — Access ChatGPT on any webpage without switching tabs.
  2. Perplexity — AI search from any page. Highlight text and ask questions.
  3. Merlin AI — GPT-4 powered assistant in any website. Summarize, write, translate.
  4. Monica — AI chat, writing, and translation in your browser sidebar.
  5. Glasp — AI-powered web highlighter. Save and organize highlights with AI summaries.

Privacy & Security

  1. uBlock Origin — The best ad blocker. Lightweight and effective.
  2. Bitwarden — Free open-source password manager. Better than LastPass.
  3. Privacy Badger — Automatically blocks invisible trackers.
  4. HTTPS Everywhere — Forces secure connections on all websites.
  5. DuckDuckGo Privacy Essentials — Privacy grade for every site you visit.

Utility

  1. Dark Reader — Dark mode for every website. Easy on the eyes.
  2. GoFullPage — Full page screenshots with one click.
  3. Loom — Record screen and camera for quick video messages.
  4. Honey — Automatic coupon codes at checkout. Saves real money.
  5. Google Translate — Translate entire webpages instantly.
